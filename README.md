# eBizCard

Remix eBizCard to create your own shareable digital contact card with a QR code containing your contact details.

> eBizCard is designed for the card owner to use privately, rather than as a URL to share with everyone. It gives you two ways to show your card: open eBizCard from your phone’s Home Screen, even when offline, or tap the card to save it as an image in Photos and show that image instead.

## How to build your card

1. Click **Remix** to copy this project into your Replit workspace.
2. Open the remixed project and start a chat with the Replit Agent.
3. Copy and paste the prompt below.
4. Answer the Agent’s questions with your details and provide your website or brand assets.
5. Review the card on your phone. Tap the card to save or share the image.
6. Add it to your phone’s Home Screen or publish it when you are happy with it.

## Prompt for Replit Agent

```text
Use this remixed eBizCard app to build my personal version. Start by asking me for my name, role, company, email, phone, website, WhatsApp, LinkedIn URL, logo or app icon, other brand assets, the website URL you should use as a styling reference, and whether I want the card in portrait or landscape. Do not guess anything that I have not provided. Use my details, my preferred orientation, my icon, and the visual style of my site to update the full-screen card, readable contact details, vCard QR code, saved image, favicon, iPhone Home Screen icon, and offline version. After every iteration, check the result on a phone, make sure nothing is cut off, and bump the offline cache version so the latest update refreshes.
```

## Tips

- Provide the full LinkedIn profile URL, including `https://`.
- A logo file is helpful, but a website URL is enough to guide the visual style.
- Scan the QR code with another phone to check that all your details are included.
- If an update does not appear on your phone, open the card in Safari while online and refresh once.
